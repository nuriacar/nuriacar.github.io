  Platform-agnostic framework converting OSINT signals into quantitative risk scores via Bayesian belief update weighted by AHP trigger prioritisation (15 behavioural triggers, nine experts); real-time posterior updates, time decay, false-positive rollback and evidence chains for SOC analysts.
